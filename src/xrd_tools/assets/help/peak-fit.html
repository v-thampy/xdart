What a diffraction peak measures, and how the Peak Fitter fits it

A diffraction peak is the signature of one family of lattice planes. Its position gives their spacing, its width the size and strain of the crystallites, and its area how much of the phase is there. This guide starts with that physics, then shows how each number the Peak Fitter reports follows from it.

An .xye file's name only suggests its unit. xdart writes iq_ for q in Å⁻¹ and in nm⁻¹ alike, so the Peak Fitter waits for you to choose the unit before it reads the file.

Range, uncertainties and weights

  • Full, on by default, fits the whole trace and shows its ends greyed; turn it off to type a range, or drag the shaded range on the plot.
  • Auto, beside No. of peaks, finds up to twelve peaks in the range; turn it off to fit the count, or type centres in Peak parameters.
  • σ initial seeds each peak's width; σ min / max bound the fitted width, and Center ± delta bounds the fitted centre around its start. Max iterations caps the solver.
